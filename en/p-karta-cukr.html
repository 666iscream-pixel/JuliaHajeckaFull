Service

CUKR residence card for Ukrainian citizens in Poland

We help you prepare the documents and go through the CUKR residence card procedure: from checking the eligibility requirements to monitoring the case.

Price 650 złBook a consultation
Don't wait — submit your application before 4 March 2027!

What the service includes

  • Specialist consultation — analysis of your situation and check of the CUKR eligibility requirements.
  • Document review — identifying the required documents and checking that they are complete.
  • Application preparation — completing the application in the MOS 2.0 system and helping with electronic submission.
  • Electronic tools — help with Profil Zaufany, an electronic signature and an e-Doręczenia mailbox, if required for the procedure.
  • Case monitoring — tracking the proceedings and informing you of any necessary next steps.
  • Ponaglenie — analysis of the grounds and preparation of a ponaglenie in case of inaction or delays, if there are legal grounds.
  • Support until you receive the card — advisory support during the final stages of the procedure.

We adapt the stages of cooperation to the specifics of each case. More — how we work.
