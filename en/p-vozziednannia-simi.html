Residence card for family reunification

We help families go through the procedure to legalise their stay in Poland — from checking documents to handling the case at the office.

Price from 1300 złBook a consultation

Cost of support

The cost depends on whether the main applicant applies at the same time as the family members, or whether their case is already being processed.

Option 1. The main applicant applies for residence based on work

If one family member applies for temporary residence and work, their husband or wife and children can benefit from a special price for support with their own cases.

Spouse's application for temporary residence1300 zł
Application for a child under 18500 zł per child

Family benefit: when the cases are handled as one family process, the cost of support for the spouse and children is lower.

Option 2. The main applicant has already applied and the case is being processed

If the husband or wife has already applied for temporary residence and work and the case is being processed by the voivodeship office, the other spouse can submit a separate application for temporary residence on the appropriate legal basis.

Spouse's application for temporary residence1500 zł
Application for a child under 18500 zł per child

We will check your situation, identify the appropriate legal basis and prepare the documents in line with the procedure's requirements.

What the cost of support includes (options 1 and 2)

  • Analysis of the family's situation and of the legal basis for the application
  • Individual list of required documents
  • Review of the applicant's documents and proof of family ties
  • Preparation and completion of the temporary residence application
  • Check that the documents are complete before submission
  • Support with submitting the application within the agreed scope of services
  • Monitoring of the case and information on any necessary next steps

Legalising the stay of parents of adult children in Poland

Full support, per person1800 zł

What the service includes:

  • Preliminary analysis of the parents' situation and the legal status of the adult child in Poland
  • Checking whether there are legal grounds for a temporary residence permit due to family circumstances
  • Individual list of required documents
  • Review of documents confirming family ties, financial means, housing and other circumstances of the case
  • Preparation of the application and necessary attachments according to the chosen legal basis
  • Help with submitting the documents through the appropriate procedure
  • Monitoring of the case and information on the main procedural steps
  • Preparation of up to two standard replies to requests from the authority within the agreed scope of the service

Good to know

Reunification of parents with adult children is not a procedure available automatically on the basis of kinship alone. Before we start, the child's legal status in Poland and the specific family circumstances need to be assessed. In some cases a different legal basis for legalising the stay should be considered.

The price covers support services. State fees, translations, notary costs and other external costs are paid separately. Complex additional requests from the authority, an appeal and a court complaint are not included in the basic scope and are agreed separately.

We adapt the stages of cooperation to the specifics of each case. More — how we work.
