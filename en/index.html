Who we help

Real support for foreigners and employers in Poland

We offer specialised services tailored to individual needs. We focus on understanding your situation and providing effective, practical solutions.

For foreigners

Want to sort out your stay in Poland, deal with official matters or start your own business? We'll guide you through the formalities.

  • Residence legalisation — obtaining a temporary or permanent residence card.
  • Polish citizenship — preparing an application for the granting of or recognition as a Polish citizen.
  • Document exchange — residence cards and driving licences, as well as obtaining a PESEL number.
  • Appeals against official decisions — help when a document or permit has been refused.
  • Registration of residence for EU citizens — formalities related to living in Poland.
  • Transcription of foreign civil status records — entering foreign documents into the Polish register.
  • Profil Zaufany (Trusted Profile) — access to online public administration services.
