What does the obligation to notify the Voivode that the reason for a temporary residence permit has ceased mean?

A foreigner has 15 working days to notify the Voivode that the circumstances on which the permit was based have ceased.

„Zgodnie z art. 113 ustawy o cudzoziemcach, cudzoziemiec, któremu udzielono zezwolenia na pobyt czasowy, zawiadamia wojewodę, który udzielił tego zezwolenia, w terminie 15 dni roboczych, o ustaniu przyczyny udzielenia zezwolenia”.

Under Article 113 of the Polish Act on Foreigners, a foreigner who has been granted a temporary residence permit must, within 15 working days, notify the Voivode who granted it that the circumstances on which the permit was based have ceased.

This may be, for example, the end of employment or studies, or the end of other circumstances on which the permit was granted.

How do you notify the Voivode?

The notification must be submitted in writing to the relevant voivodeship office. It should include your personal details, the case number (if known) and information that the reason for granting the permit has ceased.

Important: the end of the reason for which the permit was granted may affect your further legal stay in Poland. It is therefore worth checking whether you have another basis for staying in the country.
