Changed your surname after marriage: can you leave Poland with your old passport and residence card?

Yes, travel may be possible on the basis of a valid passport and a residence card issued in your maiden name.

Yes, travel may be possible on the basis of a valid passport and a residence card issued in your maiden name. However, before travelling it is worth checking the border crossing rules and the conditions for returning to Poland.

Which documents should you carry when returning to Poland?

If your new passport is in your husband's surname and your residence card is still in your maiden name, it is recommended to carry:

  • a valid passport in the new surname;
  • a valid residence card in your maiden name;
  • your marriage certificate and, if necessary, its sworn translation into Polish;
  • your old passport or a copy of it, if available.

These documents will help confirm the change of surname, but they do not guarantee that you will automatically be allowed across the border.
