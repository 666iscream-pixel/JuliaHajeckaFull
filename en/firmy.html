Why us

Formalities without unnecessary stress

Employing foreigners involves a range of formalities, obligations and administrative procedures. Changing regulations, different legal bases for work and residence, and the need to submit documents on time make handling the whole process correctly time-consuming and demanding for an employer.

We support both employers who hire foreigners and the workers themselves. Our goal is to give you security, save you time and provide properly prepared documentation that meets the requirements of the relevant offices.

Scope of cooperation

How we help

01

Checking whether a foreigner can be employed

We determine which procedure applies and which documents are needed in the specific situation.

02

Legalising the work of Ukrainian citizens

Notifications of entrusting work to Ukrainian citizens — checking obligations and completing the required formalities.

03

Declaration on entrusting work to a foreigner (oświadczenie)

Preparing and submitting the declaration where this procedure applies (citizens of Armenia, Belarus, Moldova and Ukraine), and support with the further obligations related to the procedure.

04

Work permit — types A–S

Choosing the right procedure and obtaining the required documents.

05

Temporary residence and work

We handle the single temporary residence and work permit from start to finish — from preparing documents and submitting the application to obtaining the residence card. The foreigner doesn't need to organise the next stages themselves, and the employer gets support with the employment-related documents.

06

Change of employer for residence card holders

If you want to hire a worker who holds a residence card based on work, we analyse their situation and determine which formalities are needed. We also help workers who are leaving their current employer.

Documents and data

Security of the documents you entrust to us

Working with foreigners and public offices requires sharing many documents and data. Documentation provided as part of a case — including company documents, bank account details, tax information or ZUS documents — is used solely to carry out the specific procedure.

Thanks to formal cooperation and a clearly defined scope of services, you stay in control of who receives your documents and for what purpose they are used. We want our clients to have clarity about every stage of their case.
