About us

Individual approach. Experience. Strategy.

Julia Hajecka

Since 2017 I have been supporting foreigners, their families and entrepreneurs in matters of legalising residence and work in Poland. Today I combine my own experience with the knowledge of a team of trusted specialists: I set the direction, ensure a consistent strategy and maintain the quality of service at every stage.

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Before we act, we analyse the circumstances of the case, the client's needs and the available legal options — and we look beyond a single application: at long-term plans, risks and the next stages of your stay in Poland.

More than 1,500 families have trusted me with their cases. Behind this number are real stories and people for whom legalisation means being able to live together, work and plan their future in Poland.

Approach

Our principles

An individual approach to every case

We don't use ready-made templates. We carefully analyse your situation, needs and goals to propose the best solution in line with the current regulations.

From the start you know what our cooperation involves, how much it costs and how long it will take. Transparent terms, reliable information and no hidden costs.

Ongoing support and contact

We stay in touch at every stage: we answer your questions and keep you updated on progress. You can reach us by phone, email, WhatsApp, Viber and Telegram.
