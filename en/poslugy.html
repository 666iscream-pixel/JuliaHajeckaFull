Support with legalisation

Temporary residence card: support packages

Help with preparing documents, submitting the application and handling the procedure to legalise your stay in Poland.

Start Legal*

1,000 zł

For those who want to go through the procedure on their own, with professional help in preparing the documents.

  • Individual consultation on your case
  • Personalised checklist of required documents
  • Review of the document set — 1 time
  • Identification of missing documents and necessary additions
  • Preparation of the application in the MOS system
  • Preparation of documents for electronic submission

You receive a prepared set of documents and know exactly which steps you need to take yourself.

Full Support*

1,800 zł

For those who want to entrust document preparation and the main handling of the case to a specialist.

Everything in Start Legal, plus:

  • Help with collecting and organising documents
  • Preparation of documents to be provided by the employer
  • Support with the electronic submission of the application
  • Monitoring of the case status and important procedural deadlines
  • Review of the document set — 1 time before submission
  • Preparation and filing of 1 complaint about the authority's inaction (ponaglenie), if there are legal grounds
  • Preparation of up to 2 standard replies to requests from the authority
  • Preparation of up to 2 additional document sets within the case
  • Appeal, if there are legal grounds
Choose package

Premium Fast Track*

2,100 zł

For those who need extended support, in particular when the case is delayed.

Everything in Full Support, plus:

  • Review of the document set — up to 2 times before submission
  • Preparation of up to 3 standard replies to requests from the authority
  • Preparation of up to 3 additional document sets within the case
  • Analysis of the procedural situation in case of delays
  • Preparation and filing of 1 complaint about the authority's inaction (ponaglenie), if there are legal grounds
  • Help with setting up Profil Zaufany (Trusted Profile)
  • Help with setting up e-Doręczenia (e-Delivery)
  • Support when collecting the residence card, within the agreed scope

* Special terms for families

Apply for several family members at the same time and benefit from special prices:

  • Each additional adult family member — 1300 zł
  • Child under 18 — 500 zł

* General terms for all packages

  • Prices apply to standard cases.
  • State fees (list), power of attorney costs, translations and other external costs are paid separately.
  • The number of reviews means the number of checks of the document set within the agreed scope, not unlimited editing of each document.
  • A standard reply means preparing a response to a typical request within the current case. Complex requests, new circumstances or significant additional analysis are priced separately.
  • Additional work is carried out after the scope and cost have been agreed with the client.
  • A complaint about the authority's inaction (ponaglenie) or any other complaint is prepared and filed only where there are appropriate legal grounds.
  • The decision and processing time depend on the competent authority. No package guarantees a positive decision or a faster procedure.
