Can you leave Poland after applying for a CUKR residence card?

Applying for a CUKR residence card does not take away the applicant's UKR status.

You can apply for a CUKR residence card until 4 March 2027.

Can you legally work in Poland with UKR status? Employer obligations and employment rules

Ukrainian citizens with UKR status can work without a separate work permit, provided the legal requirements are met.

Changed your surname after marriage: can you leave Poland with your old passport and residence card?

Yes, travel may be possible on the basis of a valid passport and a residence card issued in your maiden name.

Your temporary residence permit is expiring. How do you “extend” it?

Poland has no procedure for automatically extending a temporary residence permit.

You did not collect a letter from the office in your residence case. What should you do?

Under certain conditions an uncollected letter is deemed delivered, even if the addressee never actually received it.

What does the obligation to notify the Voivode that the reason for a temporary residence permit has ceased mean?

A foreigner has 15 working days to notify the Voivode that the circumstances on which the permit was based have ceased.
