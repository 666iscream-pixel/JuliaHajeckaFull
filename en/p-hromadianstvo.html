Polish citizenship

We handle the procedure for recognition as a Polish citizen by the Voivode — for foreigners who meet the statutory requirements.

Price from 3000 złBook a consultation

About the service

Every situation is individual. We analyse your residence history, check your documents and determine whether you meet the statutory requirements.

What our support includes

  • Analysis of your situation — we identify the appropriate legal basis.
  • Document review — we assess your residence history, income, housing and other required circumstances.
  • Application preparation — we prepare the application and the necessary attachments.
  • Case handling — we monitor the procedure and help you respond to the authority's requests within the agreed package.
  • Support until the decision — we accompany you through all stages set out in the contract.

Who is this service for?

We help assess whether you can obtain citizenship, in particular on the basis of long-term residence in Poland, marriage to a Polish citizen, Polish descent or the Pole's Card, refugee status, and in other cases provided for by law.

Your path to Polish citizenship starts with a proper assessment of your situation.

We adapt the stages of cooperation to the specifics of each case. More — how we work.
