title: "Family reunification: a 3-year residence card for the applicant's mother"
lead: The applicant's mother received a 3-year residence card, even though the applicant had no income of her own.
situation: |
  A woman asked us to help legalise her mother's stay in Poland. The applicant herself held a resident permit but had no income or property of her own, as she was fully supported by her husband.

  This situation required an individual approach: it was not enough to collect a standard set of documents — the family circumstances had to be properly substantiated and the family's financial situation confirmed. In such cases the list of documents is not always obvious, and the office's standard guidance may not be enough to understand how best to prove specific circumstances.
done: |
  - We analysed the applicant's personal and family situation.
  - We determined which circumstances needed to be proven for the case.
  - We prepared an individual set of documents, taking into account that the applicant had no income of her own and was supported by her husband.
  - We prepared documents that properly reflected the family's financial situation and substantiated the grounds for legalising the mother's stay.
result: |
  The applicant's mother received a residence card valid for 3 years.
moral: This case shows that in family residence cases it is important to consider not only the formal list of documents, but also real-life circumstances. An individual analysis helps identify what evidence is needed in your particular situation, avoid unnecessary steps and reduce the risk of delays caused by insufficient documentation.
