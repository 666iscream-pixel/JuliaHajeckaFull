tag: Employer
title: "12 specialists in 2 weeks: comprehensive support with employment and legalisation"
lead: All 12 specialists started working for their new employer within two weeks.
situation: |
  An employer came to us who needed to hire 12 specialists — citizens of Ukraine and Belarus. Each worker had an individual migration situation: some already held a residence card issued in connection with work for a previous employer; some had a residence application pending; some were only preparing to apply, and two had UKR status.

  An additional difficulty was that most of the specialists had families, so a change of job could affect not only their own situation but also the further legalisation of their relatives' stay.

  There was little time to organise the process, while the workload and responsibility were considerable. We had to quickly analyse 12 separate situations, determine the appropriate procedures and prepare the documents so that each worker could start work in compliance with the law.
done: |
  - We carried out an individual analysis of the migration status of each of the 12 workers, taking into account existing residence cards, pending proceedings and UKR status.
  - We assessed the employment situation and identified the documents and procedures required for each specialist.
  - We took into account the workers' family circumstances and the possible impact of a job change on the legalisation of their family members' stay.
  - We prepared individual sets of documents for employment and for subsequent applications or necessary changes, depending on each situation.
  - We coordinated the process so that the employer and the workers could act consistently, without unnecessary delays or confusion over documents.
result: |
  Within two weeks of the first consultation, all 12 specialists had started working for the new employer. Each received a set of documents prepared for their own situation for the further procedures required.

  All the workers subsequently received the required residence cards, and some of them have already applied with us for an EU long-term resident permit.
moral: This case is about systematic work with many different migration situations at once. When an employer needs to bring in specialists quickly, one standard set of documents for everyone is not enough. It is important to assess each worker's status, consider their family circumstances and correctly determine the next steps. This is the approach that makes it possible to organise the process quickly and reduce the risk of errors.
