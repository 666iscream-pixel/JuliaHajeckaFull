title: "Residence card in 18 days: resolving a case urgently before travelling abroad"
lead: After five months of waiting, the applicant received her residence card 18 days after we reviewed the case.
situation: |
  A woman came to us who had personally submitted a complete set of documents for a temporary residence permit based on marriage. The case was dragging on: she had been waiting for the Voivode's decision for more than five months.

  The situation became particularly urgent because she needed to travel abroad, and the uncertainty about when the case would be completed made it difficult to plan the trip.
done: |
  - We reviewed the documents and the case files.
  - We analysed the current state of the proceedings and possible further steps to speed up the decision.
  - We organised further handling of the case, taking into account the applicant's urgent need for a document confirming the permit.
result: |
  Just 18 days after we reviewed the documents in the case, the applicant received her residence card.
moral: This case shows that even after a long wait it is important to assess the actual state of the case and identify the next steps that can help bring it to a close.
