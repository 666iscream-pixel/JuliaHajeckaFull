Ukrainian citizens who benefit from temporary protection in Poland and meet the requirements can apply for a CUKR residence card. This document allows them to move from UKR status to a temporary residence permit on special terms.

## What is the deadline for a CUKR application?

You can apply for a CUKR residence card until 4 March 2027. Applications are submitted exclusively online via the dedicated MOS portal.
