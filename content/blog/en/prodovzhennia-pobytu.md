---
title: Your temporary residence permit is expiring. How do you “extend” it?
date: 2026-10-12
lead: Poland has no procedure for automatically extending a temporary residence permit.
---
Poland has no procedure for automatically extending a temporary residence permit. To continue living in the country legally, you must apply for a new permit before your current one expires.

If you do not plan to stay in Poland, you must leave before your permit expires, unless you have another basis for legal stay.
