If a letter was properly sent to the address given in your application but was not collected in time, it is returned to the office marked “not collected within the time limit”. Under certain conditions such a letter is deemed delivered, even if the addressee never actually received it.

## What should you do in this situation?

Find out which letter was sent and whether it set a deadline for meeting the office's requirements.

If the letter did not concern correcting formal defects in your application, you can contact the office via its form or submit a written explanation asking for the deadline to be extended.

**Important:** not collecting correspondence does not release you from the obligation to submit documents. If the letter contained a request to correct formal defects, you need to act according to its content and check whether the deadline can still be met.
