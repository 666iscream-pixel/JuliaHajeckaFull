---
title: Can you legally work in Poland with UKR status? Employer obligations and employment rules
date: 2026-10-16
lead: Ukrainian citizens with UKR status can work without a separate work permit, provided the legal requirements are met.
---
Ukrainian citizens who benefit from temporary protection in Poland and hold a PESEL number with UKR status can work without obtaining a separate work permit, provided the legal requirements are met.

The right to work allows employment under an employment contract (umowa o pracę) or a civil-law contract (umowa zlecenia), as long as the specific form of employment complies with the applicable rules. Special statutory restrictions may apply to certain positions.

## What must the employer do?

For the employment to be lawful, the employer must complete a number of formalities:

- Notify the district labour office (Powiatowy Urząd Pracy) that work has been entrusted to a foreigner, following the established procedure.
- Meet the notification deadline — as a rule, within 7 days of the start of work.
- Sign an appropriate contract setting out the working conditions, pay and duties.
- Fulfil ZUS and tax obligations, in particular registering the employee for social insurance where required for the type of contract and the employee's status.

## What if the employer does not notify the labour office on time?

The obligation to notify about the employment lies with the employer, and so does the liability for failing to do so.

At the same time, the legal consequences of a missing notification must be assessed in light of the applicable rules and the specific circumstances of the employment. You should not assume that every breach automatically has no consequences for the employee: it is important to check whether they still have a legal basis to stay and work.

An employer who breaches the notification requirements may face a fine. That is why completing the formalities on time remains an important part of lawful employment.

## Do you need a work permit?

If you have the right to work based on UKR status, a separate work permit is usually not needed. However, you should check that your status is current, your stay is legal and the specific form of employment meets the applicable requirements.

If you have lost UKR status or your basis of stay has changed, different rules on access to the labour market may apply.
