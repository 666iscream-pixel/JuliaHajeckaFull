---
title: Can you leave Poland after applying for a CUKR residence card?
date: 2026-10-20
lead: Applying for a CUKR residence card does not take away the applicant's UKR status.
---
Applying for a CUKR residence card does not take away the applicant's UKR status. This means that while waiting for the card you can travel outside Poland, but a single absence must not exceed 30 days.

## What should you pay attention to?

- **Travelling abroad:** after applying you can travel, as long as you respect the limit on the length of absence.
- **UKR status:** applying for CUKR does not in itself cause you to lose it.
- **Receiving the CUKR card:** once you collect the card, your UKR status ends and your stay in Poland is then based on the temporary residence permit granted in connection with that card.

## Why is it important to control the length of your trip?

If you plan to travel while your application is being processed, check your travel dates in advance and make sure your absence does not exceed the permitted period. Breaching the rules of stay under UKR status may affect your legal status and further legalisation.
